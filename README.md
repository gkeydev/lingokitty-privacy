# Privacy Policy for Lingo Kitty

**Last updated: October 13, 2026**

Lingo Kitty ("we", "our", or "the game") is an offline educational game developed by Lajos Kelemen, designed to help players learn vocabulary and languages through interactive 2D gameplay.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

---

### 1. No Personal Information Collection
Lingo Kitty **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information**.
- We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or locations.
- We do not access device contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or personal files.
- We do not collect telemetry, diagnostic data, or user behavior analytics.

### 2. Offline Gameplay & Local Data Storage
Lingo Kitty functions **100% offline**. All game progress, level unlocks, and language preferences are stored **strictly locally on your device**. No data is ever transmitted to external servers, cloud services, or third parties.

### 3. No Advertising or Tracking
Lingo Kitty contains:
- No third-party advertising networks.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No in-app profiling or tracking cookies.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Lingo Kitty is family-friendly and suitable for all ages, including children. Because the application collects zero personal information, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
